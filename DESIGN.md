# Folio design system
## 0. Research Log
User PRD is the visual authority: tactile stacked weekday sheets, orange completion. Type revised to Instrument Serif (display) + Instrument Sans (UI) after the user asked for modern, elegant, less overused faces. Distill pass (user brief): the stack is the whole interface. No header, footer, hero, flavor copy, settings page or theme switcher. Wheel pickers adapt the geometry and inertia of @ncdai/react-wheel-picker (MIT) to Svelte.
## 1. Direction
Operate mode. A personal paper folio, not a dashboard. Oversized day names are the navigation. The open sheet lifts from a seven-sheet stack; Product idea: build the ideal repeating list once (daily, a few days a week, weekly), land on today, check things off; repeats come back fresh tomorrow. Orange marks today, completion and the primary action. One floating orange button adds; tapping a task edits it.
## 2. Color
Light: canvas #eae9e4, paper #faf9f6, sheet #e2e1dc, ink #292924, muted #62625b, orange #e94d20, orange text #ae3410, soft orange #fce6dc, line #dddcd5, field #f0efea. Dark: canvas #171816, paper #272924, sheet #20221e, ink #f3f3ed, muted #b2b5aa, orange #ff7950, line #363932, field #30332d. Primary action: light #b23d16 with white text, dark #ff7950 with #1d1f1b. Selected day dots and chips use soft orange with orange text; switches use the primary. No white or ink fills for active states. Theme follows the system only.
## 3. Typography
Self-hosted Instrument Serif 400 for weekday names (36 collapsed / 56 open on phones, 40 / 68 desktop) and the composer task name (34). Instrument Sans Variable for UI (16.5 tasks, 16 rows, 15 week title at 550, 13 metadata). Tabular times. No eyebrows, no flavor text.
## 4. Geometry
Content max 640px, 12px phone gutters. Day sheets radius 20px, 78px collapsed. Composer rows grouped in one 18px-radius field with inset hairlines, 54px rows. Bottom sheet radius 28px on phones, centered 460px card on desktop. Floating add button 62px. Touch targets at least 44px.
## 5. Primitives
Week bar: arrows plus a relative title (This week / Next week / Last week / range); a Today pill appears only away from the current week. Day sheet: name, date, count or done check. Task row: square checkbox, title, optional note line, time and repeat glyph; long-press (touch) or drag (mouse) to move between days or reorder. Composer: serif task name, then an always-visible Repeat card (summary, seven day dots, Once/Daily/Weekdays/Weekends; new tasks default to Daily), then rows Time (switch + hour/minute wheels), Starts/Date (wheel), Ends (switch + date wheel, repeating only), Note. The save button names the result (Add to every day / Add to Sundays / Add to 3 days / Add task) and sits in a pinned footer outside the scroll area. Recurring edits use a sliding segmented control: This day / This & upcoming. Delete is a two-tap confirm.
## 6. Motion
Authored moment: on first load the seven sheets deal in from below, staggered 45ms. Supporting motion: open sheet slides its body (360ms quint-out) while the day name grows; week changes slide the stack horizontally in the travel direction; tasks flip into new positions and slide in/out; checkbox squeezes, fills and draws its check while a strike line sweeps the title; long-press lifts a tilted preview; composer sheet springs up, follows the finger on swipe-down and exits faster than it enters; rows expand with slide; switches, chips and day dots compress on press. Reduced motion collapses durations to near-zero.
## 7. Responsive
Same vertical week at all sizes. Sheet scrolls independently, its footer stays reachable above the keyboard via visual-viewport tracking. Floating button aligns to the content column on wide screens.
## 8. Accessibility and debt
Checkbox boundaries #888880 / #82877a (3:1 on paper). Wheels are focusable spinbuttons (arrows, Page Up/Down, Home/End). Native dialog focus management restores the opener. Dragging has a non-pointer alternative: change the date in the composer. Export/import, sync sign-in and reminder preferences have no UI after the distill pass; their modules remain for a future decision. Real-device install QA remains a deployment gate.
